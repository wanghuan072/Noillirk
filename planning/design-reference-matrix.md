# Online design-method review

Observed 2026-10-20 through opened current pages; no code, artwork, descriptions or layout copied.

| Site | Observed method | Adopt as a principle | Reject for this project |
|---|---|---|---|
| https://playdaily.org/noillirk | Play area precedes explanatory sections; mode/start controls carry immediate task | Keep action before long article, clear separation of validity and scoring | Do not reproduce pixel scenes, official brand, prose, account or shop systems |
| https://poki.com/ | Dense discovery links and many small game choices | Compact, scannable game titles in discovery areas | Do not adopt heat rankings, huge catalog, filters or copied card art |
| https://www.coolmathgames.com/ | Distinct New and Featured editorial groups; skip navigation and category links | Separate editorial group purposes and clear skip navigation | Do not adopt extensive category chrome, store links or unsupported popularity claims |
