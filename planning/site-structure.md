# Draft site plan

The original main game owns `/` only. Home, Guides and More Games share one navigation config. No topic has sufficient independent evidence. Use English and no trailing slash. Footer Legal uses `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/copyright`, `/about`, `/contact`. No author or company identity is invented.

The player, six recommendations, full-width left-column article and final video area remain in the left rail. Featured/New remain in a right two-column card rail. Container maximum: 1400px. At 1024px retain both rails with hamburger navigation; at 768px use a single column in player/recommendations/article/video/featured/new order. A tall sidebar must follow scroll direction inside its parent without an internal scrollbar. Guides with four or more sections receive a sticky scrollable table of contents.

Eight game IDs are provisional implementation targets, not eligible publication targets. All lack final runtime, video and media approval. No published game or Guide is promised. User authorizes downstream development/local builds while main-media rights remain open. Local previews must be noindex, bound to localhost, clearly distinguished from release builds and excluded from deployable acceptance.

No unlicensed screenshot, borrowed cover, other-game fallback, imaginary reviewer or copied description may enter the implementation. Image-free development surfaces are diagnostic only, not accepted production cards or player covers.
